FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week: Show Up. Build. Pitch. WIN!

Event Recap: FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week: Show Up. Build. Pitch. WIN!

Event Objectives

  • Review the teams’ journeys after Agentic AI Build Week through presentations of their real-world projects.
  • Introduce applications that combine Agentic AI with Amazon Web Services (AWS) to solve business problems.
  • Share experiences in building AI products from an idea to an MVP within a short period.
  • Present system architectures, development processes, and lessons learned from deploying AI on AWS.
  • Create opportunities to learn from the participating teams and discuss Agentic AI development trends.

Presenting Teams

  • Team 3KA – Hackathon Journey: S.H.E.P.H.E.R.D
  • One Team – Colonel AI: AI-Powered Conversation Ordering
  • Plan V – Solution Architect Professional AI Native App
  • SignalScout Team – SignalScout

Main Content

Hackathon Journey – Team 3KA

  • Shared the team’s Hackathon journey, from developing the initial idea to completing the product.
  • Introduced S.H.E.P.H.E.R.D, a system that applies Computer Vision and Agentic AI to crowd monitoring.
  • Used YOLO, ByteTrack, Amazon SageMaker, Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, and a React Dashboard to build the system.
  • Discussed the challenges of working with AI and AWS for the first time while developing an MVP under tight time constraints.
  • Shared lessons about teamwork, time management, and preparation before participating in a Hackathon.

Colonel AI – AI Conversation Ordering

  • Introduced an AI-powered food ordering system operating through messaging platforms such as Zalo and Messenger.
  • Analyzed the challenges of AI-based ordering, including natural language processing, shopping cart management, and business rules.
  • Presented an Agentic AI architecture integrated with Amazon Bedrock to automate planning, tool calling, and order confirmation.
  • Designed the system using a Microservices architecture so that it can easily scale across different communication channels and businesses.
  • Shared results related to deployment costs, performance, and system scalability.

Solution Architect Professional AI Native App

  • Presented an AI solution that assists Solution Architects in analyzing customer requirements.
  • Applied AI to read documents, generate system architectures, create Draw.io diagrams, and estimate AWS costs.
  • Allowed users to interact with the AI to revise and finalize project architectures.
  • Helped reduce design time and improve the productivity of Solution Architects.

SignalScout

  • Introduced an AI system that helps businesses identify early signals of changes in strategy and operations.
  • Collected data from multiple sources, analyzed it with AI, and displayed the results on a Dashboard.
  • Provided evidence-based analysis to support managerial decision-making.
  • Presented an architecture using Amazon Bedrock, Lambda, DynamoDB, Amplify, and other AWS services.
  • Analyzed deployment costs and proposed a more optimized architecture.

Knowledge Gained

Agentic AI

  • Understood how to build AI Agents that can independently plan, call tools, and support decision-making.
  • Understood the role of Agentic AI in automating business processes.
  • Recognized the differences between traditional Chatbots and AI Agents.

AI Combined with AWS

  • Learned how Amazon Bedrock AgentCore can be used to build AI Agents.
  • Understood the role of Amazon SageMaker in deploying AI models.
  • Learned how to combine AWS services to build a complete AI system.
  • Explored modern Cloud architectures for AI deployment.

AI Product Development

  • Understood the process of developing an MVP within a short period.
  • Learned how to divide tasks and collaborate effectively among team members.
  • Learned the process of designing, deploying, and presenting a product to a judging panel.

AI Applications in Business

  • Understood how AI can be applied to crowd monitoring, intelligent ordering, Solution Architect assistance, and business data analysis.
  • Learned how AI supports automation and improves productivity across various fields.
  • Recognized the role of AI in enterprise digital transformation.

Applications to My Work

  • Research and build AI Agent systems on AWS.
  • Explore Amazon Bedrock and new AWS AI services.
  • Apply the MVP model to personal or academic projects.
  • Improve Cloud architecture design and AI system development skills.
  • Strengthen teamwork, time management, and product presentation skills.

Event Experience

Attending FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week: Show Up. Build. Pitch. WIN! gave me the opportunity to observe various AI projects built on AWS and gain a clearer understanding of the process of developing a product from an idea into a complete demo. The teams not only introduced their technical solutions but also shared practical experiences, challenges they encountered, and how they resolved them within a very short time.

Learning from the Presenting Teams

  • Explored various ideas for applying AI to real-world business problems.
  • Understood how the teams built and deployed AI Agents on AWS.
  • Learned from their experiences of developing MVPs under tight time constraints.
  • Gained additional perspectives on system architecture and product scalability.

Technical Experience

  • Observed various Agentic AI architectures using Amazon Bedrock, SageMaker, and other AWS services.
  • Understood how Computer Vision, AI Agents, and Cloud Computing can be combined to build intelligent solutions.
  • Learned how Microservices systems and business-oriented Dashboards are designed.
  • Explored different approaches to optimizing cost and performance when deploying AI in the Cloud.

Networking and Knowledge Exchange

  • Had the opportunity to hear practical experiences from the teams participating in Build Week.
  • Gained a better understanding of AI product development in a real-world environment.
  • Expanded my knowledge of Agentic AI, AWS Cloud, and modern system architectures.

Lessons Learned

  • A complete MVP always delivers more value than an ambitious idea that has not yet been implemented.
  • AI Agents are becoming an important trend in software development and business automation.
  • AWS provides a comprehensive ecosystem for building modern AI applications.
  • The success of a project depends not only on technology but also on teamwork, time management, and the ability to communicate ideas.
  • The practical experiences shared through these projects motivated me to continue learning and developing my skills in AI, AWS, and Cloud Computing.
